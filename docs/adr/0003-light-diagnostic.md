# 3. Keep the placement diagnostic light

Date: 2024-03-18
Status: Accepted

## Context

The first-session experience decides whether a student comes back. In the pilot
classrooms, a 25-question placement test made students anxious and pulled down day-2
retention.

## Decision

- At most 12 items in total and at most 3 per skill. A skill stops early after two
  consistent answers.
- Items are chosen at the difficulty with the most information (p ≈ 0.5).
- No score is shown. The summary says where the student will start, never what they got wrong.

## Consequences

Placement is coarser than a full test. That's acceptable, because practice keeps adapting
from the first item. Getting a student practising quickly matters more than a precise placement.
